How to create and send a quotation
  1. Create draft
    Choose the client and whether this is a chemical or treatment-plant quotation.
  2. Enter pricing and terms
    For chemicals, select the recipe, quantity, currencies, conversion and margin. For treatment plants, enter the total selling price.
  3. Finish
    Save, mark ready, download the Excel quotation + PI, then record it as sent.
